The slab stamps the numbers
Same two families, but instead of Chivo quietly absorbing the mono's work, Alfa Slab One takes it over and makes it loud. Durations, keys, BPM, vote counts and chord symbols all become stamped gold marks — the numbers stop being metadata and start being label copy.

Wins
The most characterful of the three, and it solves the hierarchy problem A1 creates — a duration is now visibly a different kind of thing from a sentence.
Costs
Density. Everything numeric has to be big, so lists get taller and a rehearsal no longer fits one phone screen. It is also the loudest — a lot of gold slab per view.
