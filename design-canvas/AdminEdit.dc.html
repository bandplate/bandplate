The drawer row
The open editor is a full-width strip under the record it belongs to, escaping the columns entirely — labelled fields on one line, a footer with Save and Cancel, and a gold marker down the left of both so it is obvious which record is open. The bet: an edit belongs to a row, and a strip under that row says so without hiding the list you are comparing against. The cost: opening one pushes every row below it down the page, and only one can sensibly be open at a time.
