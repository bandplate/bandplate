The record sheet
Editing opens the same sheet the takes filters already use — off the right on a desktop, up from the bottom on a phone — titled with the record and footed with Save and Cancel. The bet: the machinery, the animation and the dismissal rules exist and are already debugged, and one pattern then covers members, instruments and tokens no matter how many fields each has. The cost: it is modal. You lose sight of the roster while you edit, which is the one thing the table was kept for.
