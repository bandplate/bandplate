Sleeve
Not centred and not split — the screen is the back of a record sleeve. The wordmark sits top-left like a spine, a hairline runs the full width under it as the seam, and everything else hangs off the bottom-left: one display line that says what you are signing into, and the field drawn as a rule you type on rather than a box you fill. The bet: the take detail hero and the event ledger already read like print, and this is the only screen with enough room to say so properly. The cost: an underline field is less obviously a field than a filled box, and the whole thing leans harder on the type than either alternative — it looks wrong if the display face fails to load.
