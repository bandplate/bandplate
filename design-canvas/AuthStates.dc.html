Drawn in L1; they redraw in whichever direction wins, since all three share the same column. Two things worth arguing about: the sent state replaces the form rather than sitting above it — the page has done its job and asking again is not the next thing you want — and every dead end carries exactly one way forward, because a login screen that leaves you with only a sentence is a login screen you close.
