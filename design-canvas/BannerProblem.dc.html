The icon is a typed character. "i", "✓", "!" — set in the body face beside a display-face heading, at whatever size the text happens to be. Every other icon in this app is drawn SVG on a 16/20/24 grid.

Nothing limits the measure. One short sentence stretches the full width of the page, so the eye has to travel the whole bar to find out there is nothing else there.

The action is stranded. "Put it back" sits under the text at the far left of an otherwise empty block, and the emptiness reads as a mistake rather than as space.
