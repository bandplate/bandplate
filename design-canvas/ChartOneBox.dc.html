E2 — One box, the way a chart is written

Drop the two boxes for one. A section is a block: label on its own line, chord line, then the words. It is how a chart is written on paper, and there is only one rule to learn instead of two contradictory ones.

Costs: the app has to split that back into the two database columns on save, and re-join them on open — a round trip that must be lossless for text nobody structured. Pasting a chart from elsewhere no longer just works: it has to be in this shape.

Chords & lyrics

A label on its own line, the chords beneath it, then the words. Blank line between sections.

Verse Am Dm7 Život si mě ubalil, pomalu mě teď kouří. Zlehýnka mě zapálil, a já si teďka hořím. Refrén F C G Hoří, hoří, hoří.

One rule, and the thing on screen already looks like the thing that comes out. Nothing to pair, so nothing to mis-pair.

The catch is underneath: the schema keeps chords and lyrics as two columns — the ingest contract and the take pages both read them separately — so this box has to split cleanly on every save and rebuild on every open. A song whose text does not fit the shape has to survive that round trip untouched.

It also changes what a paste means. Today you can paste a chord sheet into one box and a lyric sheet into the other, from two different places. Here there is one box and one shape.
