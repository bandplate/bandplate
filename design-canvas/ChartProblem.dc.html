What the two boxes actually want

The parser takes TWO DIFFERENT conventions, one per box, and neither box says so. That is the whole bug — you can type something perfectly reasonable and get nothing back, with no way to tell why.

What is on screen today
Chords

Pasted as-is. Section labels like Verse or Refrén line the chords up with the lyrics.

Verse Am Dm7
Lyrics
Verse Život si mě ubalil, pomalu mě teď kouří. Zlehýnka mě zapálil, a já si teďka hořím.

This is what was typed into the real editor. It looks right, and it produces nothing — the song page falls back to two plain blocks with no sections at all.

The chord box wants a colon. Verse: Am Dm7 — one section per line, label, colon, changes. A label alone on its own line is not a label here; it is just text.
The lyric box wants the opposite. The label goes alone on its own line, with the words beneath it — no colon.
Nothing says either. The hint mentions labels but not the shape of one, and the Lyrics field has no hint at all.

And there is no feedback. You type, save, navigate to the song, and discover it did not work — with nothing telling you which of the two rules you missed.

What it wanted — the same song, correct

The parser already knows the band's language — Sloka, Refrén, Most, Mezihra, Předehra, Dohra all work, accents or not. Nothing on screen mentions that either.
