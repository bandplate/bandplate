E3 — Sections as rows, no format at all

Stop asking anyone to learn a text convention. A section is a row: a name, its chords, its words. Add a section, drag to reorder. The app writes the text.

Costs: the most machinery of the three, and the least forgiving of what people actually have. Pasting a whole chart is hostile — you would be splitting it into rows by hand — and the parser's deliberate free-text fallback, which exists so an unstructured song still renders, has nowhere to live.

There is no format to get wrong. No colon rule, no label-on-its-own-line rule, no pairing — the row IS the pairing.

What it costs is everything that is not already in rows. The parser today degrades on purpose: a song in a convention it does not recognise still renders as plain text rather than being mangled. Rows have no room for that, so a song like it either loses its shape or needs an escape hatch — which puts the free-text box back beside the rows and gives you two editors instead of one.
