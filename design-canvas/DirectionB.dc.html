Why this one
Condensed caps fit far more on a phone than any wide face, so a whole rehearsal is one screen with no scrolling. Acid green is the loudest possible accent that still clears 14:1 on black — every state is legible in a dark room at a glance.
The tradeoff
It is a strong flavour that will not fade into the background, and the neon reads more dancehall than roots. Condensed caps are also poor for Czech song titles with heavy diacritics — háčky crowd the cap-height.

Motion
Rows slide in from the left on a 30ms stagger, like a flyer being printed line by line. The acid rail on the playing row is the only thing that ever animates twice.
Atmosphere
A 9px diagonal hatch — screen-print misregistration — over a green wash from the top and a siren glow bottom-right. Square corners everywhere; nothing is rounded.
Desktop
Density is the point: at 1440px the take list becomes a real table with eight columns, condensed caps holding it together, and the hatch spans the full ground.
