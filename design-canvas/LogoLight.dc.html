The same twelve on bone
Identical geometry, different ink. Each strip compares gold, burnt gold and solid ink so you can see why the mark cannot simply be recoloured.
01 Groove
Concentric grooves and a spindle hole. The most literal read of a plate — and the most generic, because every music app since 2010 has drawn a circle.
goldburntink16
02 Spindle
The centre hole and nothing else. Survives 16px better than anything here and stays a ring at any weight, but it is a ring — it carries no idea on its own.
goldburntink16
03 Stack
Three plates seen edge-on. The only mark in the first six that says *archive* rather than *audio*, which is what the app actually is.
goldburntink16
04 B-plate
A slab B whose bowls are plates. Ties the mark to the wordmark and to Alfa Slab One, so the lockup reads as one drawing rather than a badge beside a name.
goldburntink16
05 Cut
Chosen and shipped. The cutting head striking in toward the centre. A dubplate is cut, once, for one sound — this shows the verb the app is named after. Shown here in its final form: the groove ring is gone, so one drawing serves the header and the 16px favicon alike.
goldburntink16
06 Sleeve
A sleeve with the label die-cut out. Square, so it fills a favicon edge to edge instead of floating in it.
goldburntink16
07 Echo
A plate with two fading repeats behind it. This is dub's actual signature — tape delay — rather than vinyl in general, and it reads as *many takes of one song*.
goldburntink16
08 Cabinet
A sound-system speaker box with two drivers. Nothing else here says reggae this fast, and it is the only mark about the room the music is played in rather than the medium.
goldburntink16
09 Skank
The offbeat chop, drawn as it is felt: nothing on the beat, weight on the and. The most reggae-specific mark here and the only abstract one — and bars survive any size.
goldburntink16
10 Lowercase b
A slab b whose bowl counter is the spindle hole. Quieter than 04 and it sits better beside a lowercase wordmark, which is the default lockup.
goldburntink16
11 Spiral
One continuous groove from rim to centre — a single unbroken take. The most elegant at large sizes and the first to collapse at small ones.
goldburntink16
12 Filed
A plate three-quarters into its sleeve. The archive idea from 03 done as an object instead of a diagram, and it keeps a square silhouette.
goldburntink16
Gold does not survive here. At 88px it is a warm mark; at 16px it is a smudge on bone — 2.1:1, the same measurement that demoted gold to a fill colour in the light palette. The light mark is burnt gold #7A4E05, and the favicon should be the ink version, which holds at any size on any ground. The two-tint marks (03, 07, 09, 12) lose the most in single colour — check those against their 1-colour rendering on the dark board before choosing one.
