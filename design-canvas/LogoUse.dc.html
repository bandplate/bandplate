What the 16px row settles
01 loses its rings to a dot. 03 and 07 smear. 11 fills in solid — the spiral is a large-size mark only. 02, 05, 06, 08, 09 and 12 hold — 05 only after losing its groove ring, which is why the shipped mark has none — and the square-silhouette ones (06, 08, 12) read largest at the same pixel count because they fill the tab rather than float in it.
Where I'd land
05 Cut still leads on meaning — it shows the verb the name is built on. 09 Skank is the real new challenger: the offbeat is reggae's actual signature, it is the only mark here nobody else could have drawn for a different app, and it is indestructible at any size — but it says rhythm, not plate. 08 and 12 are the pick if you want the mark to be instantly legible as what it is. One mark, three lockups — never two marks.
