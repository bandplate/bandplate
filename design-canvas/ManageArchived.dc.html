Three facts the member cannot get anywhere else: what disappears, what survives, and that it is reversible — twice over, by hand and by the bridge.

"Its takes still turn up in search" is the one that reads as a bug if unsaid. Archiving is a repertoire decision — we do not play this any more — not a decision about the recordings.
