A — The last row

Adding lives at the END of the list, as a dashed row the same height as a song row. The toolbar stays what it is: a way to find things. The list stays what it is: the repertoire, with one more slot at the bottom.

Costs: invisible until you scroll. On a 60-song library the one action a new member most needs is the one they never see.
