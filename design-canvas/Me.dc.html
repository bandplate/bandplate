M4 — Me
Your own page
Today it is four sections at one weight — an identity CARD (the only card in the app), a device list with raw user-agent strings in mono, favourites grouped under Songs / Events / Takes (the taxonomy we just rejected on home), and the vote history. All three below fix the same three things: identity becomes a line, instruments become the icons every other screen uses, and devices stop reading like a security log. They differ on the real question — does Pinned belong here at all, now that home IS the shelf?
M1 — Home’s private twin
Home’s private twin. Same plates, same shelf, because these ARE the same pinned things — one merged list, no Songs/Events/Takes headings. Identity is a line, not a card: the app has one member looking at it and it already knows who they are. Devices move to a side column, where an infrequent, technical thing belongs.
