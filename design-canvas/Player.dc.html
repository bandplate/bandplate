The plate, with a source switch
The plate keeps its disc, transport and waveform and takes P3's source control — but as two halves of one thing rather than another chip in the row. The disc is the INDICATOR: its label carries the instrument you are soloing, the way a dubplate's label is where you read what is cut on it. The chip beside the meta is the CONTROL — pressable, captioned, with a real hit target, because a 44px disc is no place to hang a list of eight stems. On a take with no stems the chip is not rendered at all (absent, not disabled) and the disc keeps its plain gold label.
