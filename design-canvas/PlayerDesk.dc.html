The desk
The bar's job is not only "is it playing" but what am I hearing — so the source is a control in it, not a thing you set on the take's page and then lose track of. A press cycles master and the stems the take has, and the rail runs under the row. The bet: soloing is the feature that makes this archive worth opening at a rehearsal ("what is the bass doing at 1:42"), and today you can only switch stems by navigating back to the take. The cost: a control that is dead on most takes — a take with no stems has nothing to switch to — and the waveform does NOT follow the switch: peaks are stored one file per TAKE (takes/{id}/peaks.json), so soloing the bass leaves you looking at the master's shape. Fixable, but that is a storage-key decision to take now rather than after the bridge has run over the whole archive.
