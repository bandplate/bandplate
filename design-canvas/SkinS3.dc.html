Label
The 7" centre label as a device: every row carries a plate on the left that encodes state by fill and ring, so the play control and the state marker are the same object. Surfaces stay bare; the plate does the work a container would.
Why
The only skin that comes from the brand rather than from a component library, and it collapses two elements into one — a row needs no badge because the plate already said it. Pills and circles echo the mark throughout.
Cost
A device has to be learned: a filled red plate meaning concert is not self-evident the way the word concert is. Needs a legend somewhere, and rounded pills everywhere can tip into soft.
