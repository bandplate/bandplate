Destructive is now a solid fill
A danger tint heavy enough to see landed at almost exactly the luminance of the secondary tint — 1.05:1 apart in dark. Cancel and Delete would differ by hue alone, which is no difference at all to a red-blind viewer sitting in a confirm dialog. So the two buttons that act take solid fills and the one that backs out stays a tint, which also separates them by weight rather than colour: 3.2:1 apart in dark.
…and carries an icon
Solid danger against solid gold measures 1.03:1 in the light theme — burnt gold and rust are nearly the same lightness. Colour cannot be the only thing marking a destructive action, so it takes a leading icon. Belt and braces on top of the brief's existing rule that a destructive confirm names the object and its consequences.
The rule the weight created
Muted text never sits on a fill. At 12% it measures 4.26:1 in light and fails. Anything on a fill takes --bp-color-fg; muted is for text on the plain ground. Placeholders are the exception and use foreground at 70%, which is a different mechanism and measures 6.5:1 or better.
