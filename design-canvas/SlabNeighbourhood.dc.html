Seven beside Zilla Slab
Contemporary slabs in the same territory as Zilla Slab, at the same sizes, with the same Czech titles and the column layout you picked. Available weights are printed on each — that is the axis that matters, since having only one is what sank Alfa Slab. Two of these have one weight too; their notes say so. The measured title width under each is real, taken from the rendered page, and tells you how much fits before a title wraps.
