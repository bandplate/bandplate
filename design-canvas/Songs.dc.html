M4 — Songs
The repertoire
All three replace the toolbar the same way — a search field and sort as pills, no Apply and no Clear, exactly as on /events. The instrument filter is gone entirely — it asked “has a take with all of these”, which is a fact about TAKES, so it belongs on /search and nowhere else. The old one was a labelled input, a select, a collapsed <details> of a dozen checkboxes and two buttons, which on a phone pushed the songs themselves below the fold. What differs is the ROW: today it carries a title, a key/tempo line and two stat pairs (“6 takes”, “last played”) in a vocabulary nothing else in the app uses.
S1 — Index
A repertoire index. The title in the slab face at row size, key and tempo in the small caps the kind labels already use, the take count on the right, a star. It is the take row’s grammar applied to a song — which is what a song list should be, since every row on it leads to a page full of take rows.

S2 — Ledger by last played
The event ledger’s grammar, with LAST PLAYED on the spine. A band mostly asks “when did we last do this one”, and this answers it before you read the title. The catch is real: sorted A to Z the spine is noise, so this direction only holds if Recently played becomes the default sort.

S3 — Plates
Songs as plates, like home’s shelf. Strongest visually and it makes the metaphor whole — but a repertoire is forty of these, they cannot carry key, tempo or a date, and alphabetical scanning across a wrapped grid is genuinely worse than down a column.
