M4 — Take detail
Where a take is judged
This is the only page that still shows a take’s state, and now the only one carrying the vote — both moved here this week. The current page treats “Your vote” as the fifth row of a definition list, between Instruments and nothing, which is why none of the three below keep that list. Instruments are the same icon run the listings use, not text tags. All three are dark; the light theme follows the same tokens.
T1 — Decision first
Action-led. The page asks two things of you in order — listen, then decide — so those two are the first blocks and they carry eyebrows that say so. The facts drop to a single quiet run (date · duration · instruments) instead of a five-row definition list, and the files move to the side. Voting stops being a metadata field sitting next to Duration.

Object-led. The take is a thing you hold: a sleeve on the left carrying the disc, its running time and what you can solo, with everything you'd say ABOUT it on the right. The vote is a full-width decision under the title. On a phone the sleeve is the top card.
