H2 — Breadcrumb

Navigation goes where everyone already looks for it: a trail across the top, song then event then here, both ancestors as ordinary links. That frees the heading to be what actually identifies this take — the date it was recorded — and collapses the header from five stacked bands to three.

Trade-off. A breadcrumb is a small target and quiet type, so the two ways out are the least prominent of the three directions. It also implies a hierarchy that is not quite real: a take belongs to a song AND an event equally, not to a song that contains an event.
