M4 — Takes, T2 on a phone
Where the filters go when there is no room
T2 is settled for desktop: a panel of labelled groups beside the results. The open question was the phone, where that panel has nowhere to sit. Two answers, and they differ in one thing — whether opening the filters keeps the results on screen. Note the CONTROLS are identical in both: a picker for the song, pills for everything else, no Apply and no Clear on desktop.

A — Opens in place
The panel unfolds and pushes the results down. Nothing is covered, the page is still one scroll, and it needs no new machinery — it is the <details> the page already uses. But five groups of pills is roughly a screen and a half, so the takes you are filtering are off-screen while you filter them.
