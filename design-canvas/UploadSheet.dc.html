U3 — An upload sheet

"Add files" opens a record sheet holding the queue, the per-file controls and the progress. The take page underneath stays a reading surface and never rearranges while bytes move.

Costs: a modal that must stay open while a process runs — close it and the upload dies. This app has otherwise avoided exactly that, and on a phone the sheet covers the take you are uploading to.
