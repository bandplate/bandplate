Already there is not an error — it is the one place the app asks before destroying something. A second lossy master lands in the same slot whatever its format, so replacing it removes the old file. A stem at a different tier is not a collision and must not warn.

Didn't finish says the whole truth. There is no resume: the storage port has no multipart by design, so a 200 MB wav that drops at 90% starts over. Better said here than discovered.

Uploading needs JavaScript. Everything else on this page works without it — editing the take, deleting a file, publishing.

The JS-off notice is the app's first admission of a JS-only capability. A presigned PUT is a cross-origin PUT with signed headers, and an HTML form can only POST multipart to its own action; the alternatives are a new signing method on the storage port with two implementations, or streaming audio through the app server, which its own header comment forbids. Deleting a file stays a plain confirm page precisely so the destructive half never depends on script.
